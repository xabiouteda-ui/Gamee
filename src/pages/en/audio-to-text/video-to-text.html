Pick a video saved on your computer or phone and get everything that's said, with timestamps. No need to convert it to audio first or upload it anywhere: it's processed in your browser.

Which videos work

The usual phone, camera and screen-recording formats: MP4, MOV (iPhone), WebM, MKV and 3GP. The tool pulls the audio out inside your browser and turns it into text; the picture isn't used.

How it works

  1. Choose the video
    Click to pick the file or drag it onto the page. On a phone, choose it from your gallery.
  2. Pick language and quality
    "Balanced" is fine for most videos; "Most accurate" for hard audio or technical terms.
  3. Download
    Plain text as TXT, or captions as SRT or VTT to upload with the video.

What about YouTube or social videos that aren't yours?

This tool works with files on your device; it doesn't download videos from other sites. For YouTube videos, YouTube itself often offers a transcript in the video's menu ("Show transcript"). Respect the copyright of what you transcribe.

From text to captions

Want the words on screen? Download the SRT and upload it to YouTube or your editor, or burn animated TikTok-style captions into the video with animated captions.

FAQ

Is my video uploaded to a server?

No. The video is read and processed inside your browser. The only download is the AI model, the first time.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, in current Android and iPhone browsers. Long videos are slower and drain the battery; for more than half an hour, a computer is better.
